About ArtemisTwin

ArtemisTwinEXPLORATION & ANALOGS

ArtemisTwin compares documented Earth analog candidates for a specific lunar or Martian test: a target, a purpose, and the places on Earth that might stand in for it.

ArtemisTwin is an open screening and educational tool, not for operational or astronaut decisions.

Terrain resemblance alone does not reproduce the lunar environment. Gravity, vacuum and radiation remain limitations.

Comparable conditions stay marked “Evidence pending” until paired, source-backed observations and a stated method exist. The list is in catalog order, not a ranking.
