What the score cannot see

Gaps & Earth limits

Unmeasured in this build

  • Ice & volatiles
  • Mineralogy index
  • Rock abundance
  • Cave proximity
  • Access & operational gates

Missing-data sensitivity: 43–54 if those conditions ranged from gap to full. This is a sensitivity check, not a confidence interval.

Reported but excluded from the fit

Surface pressure: measured at the site, but no Earth surface site can reproduce the target condition. Keeping it out avoids pretending a 101 kPa site resembles a vacuum.

Gravity, vacuum, space radiation and true polar lighting are not scored at all. They are listed in the site's Earth-limits context and cannot be fixed by choosing a different desert.
